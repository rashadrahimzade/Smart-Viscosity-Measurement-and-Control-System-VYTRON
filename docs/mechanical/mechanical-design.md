\# 📐 Mechanical Design \& Fluid Dynamics



This directory contains the 3D CAD assemblies, STEP models, technical drawings, and fluid mechanics calculations for the VYTRON system.



\---



\### 🛠️ Core Mechanical Features



\* \*\*3D CAD Modeling:\*\*

&#x20; \* Full 3D assembly modeled and verified in \*\*SolidWorks\*\*.

&#x20; \* Neutral \*\*STEP (.step)\*\* format included for universal CAD cross-compatibility.



\* \*\*Fluid Dynamics \& Sealing System:\*\*

&#x20; \* Hydrodynamic design strictly based on \*\*Stokes' Law\*\* for falling-ball viscometry.

&#x20; \* Wall-effect boundary correction factors integrated into system calibration.

&#x20; \* Custom-engineered sealing channels utilizing \*\*NBR O-Rings\*\* and \*\*Viton (FKM)\*\* seals for zero-leakage fluid containment and high chemical resistance.
