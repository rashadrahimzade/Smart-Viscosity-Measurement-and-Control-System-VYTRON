\# ⚡ Signal Integrity, Filtering \& EMI Protection



This directory documents the practical noise mitigation, hardware filtering, and Electromagnetic Interference (EMI) protection techniques implemented in the VYTRON system.



\---



\### 🛡️ Hardware Noise Suppression \& EMI Shielding Strategies



\* \*\*Relay Noise Protection (4.7kΩ Resistance Circuit):\*\*

&#x20; \* Integrated a 4.7kΩ resistor circuit on the relay control line.

&#x20; \* Prevents spurious relay triggering and floating state signal noise from inadvertently switching on the heating element during transient voltage spikes.



\* \*\*Shielded Wiring (Aluminium Foil Shielding) \& Sensor Protection:\*\*

&#x20; \* \*\*MPU-6050 Angle Sensor:\*\* Encased in an aluminum foil-shielded cable to isolate sensitive I2C signal lines from external electromagnetic interference generated by motor drivers and power supplies.

&#x20; \* \*\*Motor Wiring:\*\* Utilized aluminum-wrapped shielded cables to prevent radiated high-frequency noise from coupling into low-voltage logic circuits.



\* \*\*Motor Frame Grounding \& Shield Termination:\*\*

&#x20; \* Motor cable shield is physically connected to the NEMA 34 motor chassis and routed directly to the negative (-) power rail.

&#x20; \* Ensures induced eddy currents and switching noise are safely discharged to ground instead of floating across the circuit.



\* \*\*Ferrite Core Suppression:\*\*

&#x20; \* Installed ferrite cores (beads) on power and communication lines to absorb high-frequency common-mode EMI noise.



\* \*\*Display Pull-Up \& Dual Decoupling Capacitors:\*\*

&#x20; \* \*\*I2C Signal Line Pull-Ups:\*\* External pull-up resistors ensure sharp square-wave rise times on SDA/SCL lines.

&#x20; \* \*\*Dual Capacitor Filtering:\*\* Positioned two decoupling capacitors (ceramic for high-frequency noise + electrolytic for low-frequency ripple) right at the display power terminals to guarantee ripple-free supply voltage.
